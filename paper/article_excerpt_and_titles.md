# Excerpt and title candidates for the Diabettech article

## Excerpt (WordPress excerpt field, ~50 words)

Preferred:

A paid app promises safer AAPS tuning by tidying your data for whichever chatbot you like. I
ran its own workflow 490 times across eleven models. They read my record almost perfectly,
then disagreed about what to change, and the app stamped every answer as checked.

Alternative, punchier:

Ask once and you get an answer. Ask fifty times and you get eighteen different pairs of safety
limits, a headline that flips one time in five, and an app that accepts them all. Inside the
£4.49 app that turns your diabetes data into chatbot advice.

## Title candidates

1. Dr Digital and the Multiverse of Madness (current; standfirst carries the money line)
2. Dr Digital will see you now. And now. And now.
3. Same data, fifty answers: the app that outsources your insulin settings to whichever chatbot you open
4. The world's first digital endocrinologist. Why you should keep your money. (the original; now the standfirst)
5. A digital endo for the price of a coffee. Ask it twice and see what happens.
6. One record, eleven models, forty pairs of safety limits
7. Your settings review is one roll of the dice
8. The app that tidies your data and gambles your settings

Notes: 1 needs the standfirst to do the explanatory work, which it has; 2 is the same joke made
by repetition and would take the current standfirst unchanged; 3 and 6 are the most literal and
the most searchable; 7 and 8 carry the finding in the title at the cost of the character. If
the Marvel echo in 1 feels dated by publication, 2 keeps the persona and adds the
reproducibility point in the title itself.
